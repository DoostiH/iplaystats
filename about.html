About iPlayStats

Free beta

iPlayStats is a set of short games for learning statistics and data science at university. It's built for two things at once: helping students understand the ideas behind the tests, and helping a class feel like a team while they learn.

The games cover introductory statistics, probability, inference, regression, data science and a basic applied statistics unit. Topics follow international curriculum guidelines for statistics and data science.

Install on your phone

  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu → Add to home screen (or Install app) → choose Install, not “Create shortcut”.
  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share (square with an up arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Once installed, iPlayStats opens full-screen and works offline.
