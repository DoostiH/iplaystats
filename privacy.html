Privacy

iPlayStats free beta · last updated 11 November 2026

iPlayStats is a free set of statistics games for university students and teachers. You can use every game without an account, and we don't collect your name, email or any other personal details through the app.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a few things in your browser's local storage on your own phone or computer:

  • a random nickname (for example “Curious Quokka”) and the team you pick
  • your best score in each game
  • your answers to the class polls and your predictions
  • simple settings, such as the last unit filter you chose

This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

What other services see

  • Website hosting. Like any website, the hosting service receives standard technical information when you load the app, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep short-term logs for security and reliability.
  • Fonts. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded.
  • Feedback form. If you choose to send feedback, the form service processes what you type. Please don't include sensitive personal information.
  • Usage counts. To learn which games are used, the app counts page visits and when a game is opened or finished, using GoatCounter. GoatCounter doesn't use cookies and doesn't store personal data that identifies you; it keeps totals such as browser type, screen size and approximate country.

The app has no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Changes

Future versions may add optional accounts, class codes or live class sessions. If that changes what information is collected, this page will be updated before those features go live, and you'll be told what's collected and why.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the feedback link in the app.
